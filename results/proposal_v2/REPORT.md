**Bottom line.** The plumbing is fixed. Proposals now reach Sim3, the merge list, and once an accepted loop. But on these benchmarks they add nothing DBoW2 does not already do. In V1_01→V1_02, DBoW2 merges every run within 5.7–11.5 s of session 2, so there is no headroom. In MH_02→MH_04, where DBoW2 often fails to merge (8/12 runs unmerged), the learned ranking is far better offline. But the live node's adaptive P98 threshold, calibrated on within-session similarity (0.93–0.96), sits above every cross-session score (median best 0.83), so it never proposes. *(interpretation)* That is the one place with real headroom, and the next experiment should target it (§8).

## 5. T4: proposal recall (Gate B)

Per run with a descriptor dump (dry + injection, 3 + 3 per pair):
- **Queries:** session-2 KFs with a GT pose and ≥ 1 true session-1 revisit.
- **Learned R@1:** top-1 by cosine over all session-1 KFs.
- **DBoW2 R@3:** a true revisit is among its logged loop+merge candidates.
- **Comparable subset:** queries for which DBoW2 actually logged a candidate list.

**Only pre-merge queries are a fair comparison.** After a merge, the revisits are covisibly connected to the query, and `DetectNBestCandidates` skips connected KFs by design (`src/KeyFrameDatabase.cc:626`). DBoW2 R@3 then drops to ≈ 0 by construction. Post-merge numbers below are listed for completeness only.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | 0.5 m / 45° | pre-merge | 143 | **0.53** | 0.34 | 44 / 16 | 0.0004 |
| V1 | 1.0 m / 45° | pre-merge | 180 | 0.75 | **0.82** | 9 / 22 | 0.03 |
| V1 | 0.5 m / 45° | all (biased) | 586 | 0.43 | 0.09 | 218 / 17 | — |

Per-run V1 pre-merge (n, learned hits, DBoW2 hits): (10,10,2) (29,8,9) (16,11,2) (15,12,2) (33,10,14) (40,25,19). Learned is ahead in 4 of 6 runs.

**Caveats (in both directions):**
- R@1 for learned vs R@3 for DBoW2: this favours DBoW2.
- Learned R@1 ranks over every session-1 KF, including ones ORB-SLAM3 culled (only ~50% of session-1 KFs survive). Restricted to KFs alive at the end, V1 pre-merge R@1 changes by −0.17 to +0.10 per run (`recall_t4.json` → `learned_R@1_alivedb`, all queries, not only the DBoW subset).
- Queries are correlated within a run, so the pooled McNemar p overstates independence.
- Offline top-1 is not what the live node does: it also applies an adaptive threshold.

**Why the live node proposed nothing cross-session on MH.** For MH session-2 KFs:
- The globally best eligible match is in session 1 for 52–62% of them.
- But the best session-1 score has median 0.83, while the node's adaptive threshold has median 0.93. **0 of ~1250 session-2 KFs cleared it** across 3 runs.
- On V1: 8–12 per run cleared it (best-s1 median 0.88 vs threshold 0.95).
- The P98 threshold is learned from within-session revisit similarity, which is systematically higher than cross-session similarity, especially for the dark MH_04.

**Gate B verdict: not met.** On V1, the learned advantage exists at a 0.5 m definition and reverses at 1 m. DBoW2's candidates are correct places 0.5–1 m away, which is what Sim3 needs, and DBoW2 merges every run within seconds regardless. On MH, the learned ranking is clearly better offline (**suggestive**, n = 6 runs, correlated queries). But the proposer as built never acts on it (**confirmed**, 0 cross-session proposals in 3 runs), so no merge can be credited to it.

## 8. What would be worth doing next (not done: outside the brief, or would be tuning)

1. **Cross-map proposer.** Make the node propose the top-k session-1 (other-map) KFs with a separate, absolute or per-map-calibrated threshold, instead of one global P98 over within-session similarity. MH_02→MH_04 is the test bed: DBoW2 leaves 8/12 runs unmerged there, and the offline ranking is good. Pre-register the threshold rule before running, with ≥ 10 runs per arm (needs your OK; ~1 h per 12 runs).
2. **Fresh-map verification.** Our V1 cross-session candidates failed BoW/Sim3 because the new map had < 10 KFs. Holding a cross-map candidate and retrying it for a few KFs is a small C++ change, and it is attributable.
3. **Revisit definition.** Gate B flips between 0.5 m and 1 m. Fix one definition grounded in what Sim3 can verify (e.g. the distance of accepted DBoW2 merges, 1.8 m in MH), and state it before the next evaluation.
4. **Not worth more runs:** V1_01→V1_02 and V1_02 single-session. DBoW2 is at its ceiling there.
